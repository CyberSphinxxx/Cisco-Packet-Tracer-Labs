# Lab Implementation Plan: PTL-0044 VLAN Trunk Allowed-List Troubleshooting

## 1. Overview & Objectives
- **Lab ID:** PTL-0044
- **Title:** VLAN Trunk Allowed-List Troubleshooting
- **Category:** `switching`
- **Difficulty:** `intermediate`
- **Estimated Time:** 60 minutes
- **Scope:** Find pruning and native-VLAN mistakes without changing unrelated ports in a multi-VLAN dual-switch topology.
- **Acceptance:** Selected VLAN (VLAN 30) fails while other VLANs (VLAN 10 and 20) work across the inter-switch trunk; correcting the allowed list and aligning the native VLAN restores intended connectivity without disrupting unrelated access ports.

## 2. Topology Design
- **Switches:**
  - `SW-1`: Cisco Catalyst 2960-24TT (Distribution / Access Switch 1)
  - `SW-2`: Cisco Catalyst 2960-24TT (Distribution / Access Switch 2)
  - Inter-Switch Trunk: `SW-1 GigabitEthernet0/1` <-> `SW-2 GigabitEthernet0/1`
- **Endpoints:**
  - **VLAN 10 (Engineering / Staff):**
    - `PC-Eng1`: `192.168.10.10/24` connected to `SW-1 Fa0/10`
    - `PC-Eng2`: `192.168.10.20/24` connected to `SW-2 Fa0/10`
  - **VLAN 20 (Marketing / Sales):**
    - `PC-Mkt1`: `192.168.20.10/24` connected to `SW-1 Fa0/20`
    - `PC-Mkt2`: `192.168.20.20/24` connected to `SW-2 Fa0/20`
  - **VLAN 30 (Operations / Management):**
    - `PC-Ops1`: `192.168.30.10/24` connected to `SW-1 Fa0/15`
    - `PC-Ops2`: `192.168.30.20/24` connected to `SW-2 Fa0/15`
  - **VLAN 99 (Dedicated Native VLAN):**
    - Unused native VLAN assigned across the inter-switch trunk link.

## 3. Fault Injection & Troubleshooting Logic
### Starter Faults (`starter.pkt`):
1. **Trunk Allowed-List Pruning Error (SW-1):**
   - On `SW-1 Gi0/1`, `switchport trunk allowed vlan` is configured as `10,20,99`.
   - Fault: VLAN 30 is omitted from the allowed list on `SW-1`.
   - Symptom: VLAN 10 hosts (`PC-Eng1` <-> `PC-Eng2`) communicate successfully; VLAN 20 hosts (`PC-Mkt1` <-> `PC-Mkt2`) communicate successfully; but VLAN 30 hosts (`PC-Ops1` <-> `PC-Ops2`) fail completely with 100% loss (`Request timed out`).
2. **Native VLAN Mismatch:**
   - On `SW-1 Gi0/1`, `switchport trunk native vlan 99` is configured.
   - On `SW-2 Gi0/1`, `switchport trunk native vlan 1` remains in default state.
   - Symptom: Native VLAN mismatch triggers CDP warnings and STP port blocking.
3. **Constraint:**
   - The student must remediate the trunk without modifying or altering access ports `Fa0/10`, `Fa0/15`, `Fa0/20`.

### Remediated Completed State (`completed.pkt`):
1. **Correct Allowed-List on both switches:**
   - `SW-1(config-if)# switchport trunk allowed vlan add 30` (or `switchport trunk allowed vlan 10,20,30,99`).
   - `SW-2(config-if)# switchport trunk allowed vlan 10,20,30,99`.
2. **Aligned Native VLAN:**
   - `SW-2(config-if)# switchport trunk native vlan 99`.

## 4. Verification Tests
- **POS-01:** Inter-Switch Trunk Operational Mode: `SW-1` and `SW-2` `Gi0/1` show mode `trunk` and status `trunking`.
- **POS-02:** Aligned Native VLAN: `show interfaces trunk` on both switches confirms Native VLAN is `99`.
- **POS-03:** Comprehensive Allowed List: `show interfaces trunk` on both switches confirms `10,20,30,99` allowed and active.
- **POS-04:** VLAN 10 (Engineering) cross-switch ping (`PC-Eng1` -> `PC-Eng2`) succeeds (4/4, 0% loss).
- **POS-05:** VLAN 20 (Marketing) cross-switch ping (`PC-Mkt1` -> `PC-Mkt2`) succeeds (4/4, 0% loss).
- **POS-06:** VLAN 30 (Operations) remediated cross-switch ping (`PC-Ops1` -> `PC-Ops2`) succeeds (4/4, 0% loss).
- **POS-07:** Layer 2 Inter-VLAN Isolation: `PC-Eng1` ping to `PC-Mkt1` (`192.168.20.10`) fails with 100% loss (no routing).
- **NEG-01:** Starter State VLAN 30 Failure: In `starter.pkt`, `PC-Ops1` ping to `PC-Ops2` fails with 100% loss while VLAN 10 and 20 succeed.
- **NEG-02:** Starter State Fault Audit: In `starter.pkt`, `show interfaces trunk` confirms VLAN 30 is missing from `SW-1` allowed list and Native VLAN is mismatched (`99` on SW-1 vs `1` on SW-2).
